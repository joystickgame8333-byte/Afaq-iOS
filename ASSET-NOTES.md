# Background asset

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool for this project. The generated PNG is bundled locally at `Afaq/Assets.xcassets/DesertSunset.imageset/desert-sunset.png`; the app does not load remote images.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a portrait full-bleed photographic background asset for an elegant Arabic iPhone prayer app. Image only, absolutely no text, UI, frame, devices, icons, logos, borders or watermarks. 9:19.5 portrait composition. A vast peaceful Arabian sand desert at sunset, restrained ultra-realistic fine-art landscape photography. Top 54 percent is clean richly graded deep midnight navy sky with extremely subtle wispy clouds, a narrow dusty rose/orange luminous strip of twilight at 56 percent height. Tiny warm setting sun centered horizontally at about 57 percent image height. Far low dark mountain silhouettes on horizon. Lower 40 percent contains softly illuminated undulating charcoal-violet sand dunes with delicate realistic wind textures, foreground very dark and calm to ensure white text can overlay legibly. Color mood deep navy, muted mauve, copper sunset. Polished natural photographic lighting and atmospheric depth. No stars or moon. Not oversaturated, not dramatic fantasy, no mosque, no people, no animals, no buildings. Center focal point on sun with plenty of dark clean negative space above and below for an app interface.

`design-reference.png` is the earlier generated concept presentation. It is a design reference, not a runtime screenshot. The central design was selected by the user. Its illustrative times and dates are not authoritative.

## App icon

`Design/app-icon-source.png` was also generated using the built-in image_gen tool. `tools/prepare-assets.sh` normalizes it to the required 1024-pixel universal icon before the Xcode build.

Prompt: Create a single square opaque iPhone app icon asset for an elegant Arabic prayer time app named Afaq. No text, letters, logos, borders, pre-rounded corners or device. Deep midnight navy background, subtle atmospheric copper glow, one softly luminous orange setting sun behind two minimal flowing blue-violet desert dune silhouettes. Clean silhouette legible at small size, flat front view, no mosque, crescent, stars, ornate decoration or glass cubes.
